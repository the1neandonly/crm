# Gymliink — Brand identity

Software platform for gyms in Algeria · gymliink.com · iOS & Android.
Bilingual: French and Arabic (RTL). Premium, precise, calm.

## The logo: "Le lien ii"
The two i's of the name are drawn as two interlocked links. Their tittles are the member and the gym, connected. The member's tittle carries Volt.

## Colours
Volt `#C5F04A` (hero, under 10 %), with Volt Ink `#12160A` on top of it.

Dark neutrals:
- background `#0A0A0B`
- surfaces `#141416`, `#1D1D20`, `#2A2A2F`
- line `#26262B`
- text `#F5F5F6`, secondary text `#9A9AA3`

Light theme: background `#F4F4F2`, surface `#FFFFFF`.

States: success `#4CD98F`, warning `#F6B94F`, danger `#FF6B5E`, info `#7AA7FF`.

Proposed additions for light backgrounds:
- Volt Deep `#7FA61A`, for graphics
- Volt Text `#4E6A0A`, for text (meets AA contrast)
