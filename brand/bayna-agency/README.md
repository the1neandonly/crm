# Bayna Agency — باينة — Brand Identity Kit v2

Marketing & communication agency · Oran, Algérie

**باينة** (*bayna*) is Algerian Darija for **clear, obvious, evident**. When something can't be denied, in Oran we say one word: باينة.

| Language | Signature |
|---|---|
| English (primary) | **Make it clear.** |
| Français | **L'évidence, par le design.** |
| Darija | **خليها باينة** |

Start with **`Bayna-Agency-Brand-Guidelines.pdf`** (charte graphique, 58 pages):

- brand story, name, mission, vision, goals, values, personality, voice and audience
- logo, colours, typography (Latin and Arabic), icons, patterns, grids, charts and motion
- every application, and an index of the kit

## The logo
The symbol is a lowercase **b** built from the three shapes of a marketing report:
- a **bar** for growth
- a **pie chart** for audience and data
- a **lime slice** pulled out of the pie for the insight

The wordmark is set in Syne Bold. The Arabic name باينة is set in Readex Pro Bold.

## Before you use it
1. **Install the fonts** in `fonts/` before opening the `.pptx` and `.docx` templates.
2. **Replace the placeholders.** These are not real details:
   - name "Prénom Nom", phone, email, website (bayna.agency), Instagram handle, RC and NIF
   - sample figures and testimonial text in the templates
   - the "QR votre lien" box on the NFC card: put your own QR code there
3. **NFC chip:** the card artwork doesn't include one. Ask your printer to add an NFC chip programmed with your contact link.
4. **Printing:** Pantone references are the nearest matches. Confirm them on a physical swatch book and ask for a press proof. Bright violet `#6337FF` looks duller in standard CMYK print than on screen.
5. **Billboard:** the billboard PDF is at 1:10 scale (400×300 mm for 4×3 m). The roll-up PDF is real size.
