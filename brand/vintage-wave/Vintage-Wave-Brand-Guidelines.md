## 1. The brand

### 1.1 Our story — *Every piece has already lived.*

Vintage Wave started with a pile of forgotten jackets and a simple idea: the best clothes are often the ones already made. We hunt, clean, mend, measure and photograph each piece by hand. Then we send it out on its next wave, to someone who will love it again.

The symbol says it: **a sun rising over two waves.**
- **The first wave** is the life a piece already had.
- **The second wave** is the one it starts with you.

### 1.2 Mission — *Make second-hand the first choice.*

| # | Value | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | **Curated** | Fewer, better pieces. Every item is chosen, not dumped. |
| 02 | **Honest** | Real photos, real flaws, real measurements. No surprises. |
| 03 | **Circular** | Keep clothes in use. Buy back, resell, repair. |
| 04 | **Warm** | Hand-written notes and slow replies done well. A community, not a warehouse. |

### 1.3 Voice — *Warm, honest, a little nostalgic.*

We talk like a friend who knows clothes:
- specific about the piece
- open about flaws
- never pushy

Short sentences, real numbers, a touch of story.

**Examples**

| Situation | How we write it |
|---|---|
| Product description | Classic 80s trucker in medium stonewash. Soft from years of wear, copper buttons intact. Light fading on the collar — see photo 4. Fits like a modern M/L. |
| Instagram caption | Friday 19:00. 48 pieces, one of each. Wool for the first cold mornings, denim that already knows how to fade. Set your alarm. |
| DM reply | Hi Sara! Yes, it’s still available. Pit to pit is 56 cm, length 64 cm. I can hold it for you until tonight — shall I? |
| Sold out | All gone in 41 minutes. Thank you for giving these pieces a second life. Next drop: Friday, same time. |

| ✅ We say | ❌ We avoid |
|---|---|
| pre-loved · one of a kind · found a new home · honest wear | used · cheap · old stuff · “perfect condition” when it isn’t |

### 1.4 Who we dress — *Three kinds of treasure hunters.*

| Persona | Profile |
|---|---|
| **The style hunter** | 18–30 · wants pieces nobody else has · follows drops, shares finds |
| **The conscious buyer** | 25–40 · buys less, buys better · cares where clothes come from |
| **The collector** | Any age · knows brands and eras · checks labels, stitching, fabric |

## 2. The logo

### 2.1 Primary logo — *Symbol + wordmark*

| Element | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **The sun** | A rising sun or pearl: the find, the treasure |
| **Wave one** | The first life of the garment |
| **Wave two** | Its second life, with a new owner |
| **Wordmark** | Hand-cut capitals, kept exactly as drawn. Never retype it. |

The artwork is a faithful vector rebuild of the original logo. Use only the supplied files.

### 2.2 Lockups — *One family, six shapes*

| Lockup | Use |
|---|---|
| **Primary · stacked** | Default. Symbol above the wordmark. |
| **Horizontal** | Website header, email |
| **Badge** (round stamp: “VINTAGE WAVE · THRIFT · EST. 2026”) | Stickers, boxes, tags |
| **Symbol** | Profile picture, favicon, small spaces |
| **Wordmark + descriptor** (“THRIFT · VINTAGE · PRE-LOVED”) | Labels, shop header |
| **Wordmark** | Very small spaces |

### 2.3 Clear space & minimum sizes

**Clear space = S**, the diameter of the sun in the symbol, on every side. Nothing enters this zone.

| Lockup | Minimum size |
|---|---|
| Primary · stacked | 120 px · 30 mm |
| Horizontal | 160 px · 40 mm |
| Badge | 90 px · 22 mm |
| Symbol | 24 px · 6 mm |
| Wordmark | 90 px · 20 mm |

### 2.4 Colour versions

The logo is always **one colour** and always has strong contrast.

| Version | Background |
|---|---|
| Cream (primary) | Espresso |
| Espresso | Cream |
| Rust | Cream |
| Amber | Espresso |
| Cream | Rust |
| Espresso | Kraft |
| Black (one colour) | White |
| White | Photos (calm areas only) |

### 2.5 Misuse

Never:
- stretch or squash the logo
- rotate it
- put it on a low-contrast background (e.g. amber on espresso small, or on busy photos)
- add shadows, glows or effects
- rearrange the symbol and the wordmark
- retype the wordmark in another font
- mix two colours inside the logo

## 4. Typography

### 4.1 Typefaces — *Three voices, one tone*

| Typeface | Role | Weights |
|---|---|---|
| **Fraunces** | Headlines: soft, retro, editorial | Black 900 for headlines; SemiBold 600 *Italic* for the warm, nostalgic word |
| **DM Sans** | Text, labels, UI, prices | Regular 400 for text; Bold 700 UPPERCASE with wide tracking for labels and buttons |
| **Caveat** | Hand-written moments only | Bold 700 for prices on tags, notes, the thank-you card. Never for paragraphs. |

All three are free (SIL Open Font License) and included in `05_Polices_Fonts`.

### 4.2 Hierarchy — *From the drop to the tag*

| Level | Style | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Display | Fraunces 900 · 120/110 | **New drop.** |
| Headline | Fraunces 900 · 56/60 | **Levi’s trucker jacket** |
| Accent | Fraunces 600 italic, in Rust (on light) or Amber (on dark) | *something old is on its way* |
| Label | DM Sans 700 · +0.2em tracking · uppercase | FRIDAY · 19:00 · 1 OF 1 |
| Body | DM Sans 400 · 18/28 | Soft from years of wear, copper buttons intact. |
| Price | Caveat 700, in Rust | 2 800 DA |

**Headline formula:** one Black word or phrase plus one Italic word, e.g. “Pre-loved pieces, *new waves.*”

## 5. Graphic elements

### 5.1 The wave pattern
The symbol repeated in rows. Three colourways:

| Pattern | Use |
|---|---|
| Cream on espresso | Tissue paper, inside of the box |
| Espresso on cream | Website footer, tissue paper |

### 5.2 Paper grain
A light paper/film grain at 10–20 % over flat colour gives the vintage feel. Use it on posts, stories, packaging and covers, never on text-heavy screens.

Solid garment silhouettes in espresso inside kraft circles:

## 6. Product & listings

### 6.1 Product photography — *Five honest photos per piece*

| # | Shot |
|---|---|
| 01 | Front |
| 02 | Back |
| 03 | Label / brand tag |
| 04 | Detail or flaw |
| 05 | On body or on a hanger |

| Rule | Guideline |
|---|---|
| **Backdrop** | Kraft paper, cream linen or a warm white wall. Same backdrop for a whole drop. |
| **Light** | Daylight by a window, no flash. Shoot between 10:00 and 16:00. |
| **Framing** | 4:5 vertical, piece centred, 10 % margin. Steam everything first. |
| **Honesty** | Always photograph flaws, the label and the fabric tag. |

### 6.2 Listing formula — *Every listing, the same way*

**Title:** `Brand + item + detail · era · size`
> Levi’s trucker jacket, stonewash · 80s · M

**Description (3 sentences):**
1. What it is and why it’s special.
2. How it feels and how it fits.
3. Any flaw, with the photo number.

**Always add:** flat measurements in cm · grade · fabric · price in DA · “1 of 1”.

### 6.3 How we measure
Laid flat and buttoned, in centimetres. Customers should compare with a piece they already love.

| Code | Measurement |
|---|---|
| A | Pit to pit |
| B | Length (shoulder to hem) |
| C | Sleeve |
| D | Waist (× 2 for the full circumference) |
| E | Inseam |
| F | Hips |

### 6.4 Condition grades — *Five dots, five grades*

One shared scale on the website, tags, posts and DMs. **When in doubt, grade down.** Customers forgive honesty, not surprises.

| Dots | Grade | Description | Colour |
|---|---|---|---|
| ●●●●● | **Mint** | Never or barely worn. No signs of wear. | Sage |
| ●●●●○ | **Excellent** | Gently worn. No flaws worth mentioning. | Denim |
| ●●●○○ | **Very good** | Light, honest wear: fading or softening. | Amber |
| ●●○○○ | **Good** | Visible wear or a small flaw, photographed. | Rust |
| ●○○○○ | **Fair** | Loved hard. Flaws shown, priced to match. | Maple |

### 6.5 Pricing
- Always in **DA**, no decimals, with a thin space for thousands: `4 500 DA`.
- On physical tags and price stickers, write the price by hand (Caveat style, brown marker).

## 7. The online shop

### 7.1 Home page — *A boutique, not a warehouse*
- **Header:** horizontal logo · New drop · Women · Men · Accessories · Sold archive · About · Search · Bag
- **Hero (espresso):**
  - Label: “NEW DROP · FRIDAY 19:00”
  - Headline: “Pre-loved pieces, *new waves.*”
  - Short promise text
  - Buttons: **SHOP THE DROP** (amber) and **HOW WE GRADE** (outline)
  - Large product photo with a hand-written price tag
- **“Just in” grid:** product cards from the latest drop.

### 7.2 Product page — *Everything a buyer needs to say yes*
- **Photos:** 4–5 thumbnails and a large main photo.
- **Header block:** category · era · “1 OF 1”, then the title (Fraunces 900) and the price (DM Sans Bold).
- **Fit:** condition badge, plus size and fit note (“fits like a modern M/L”).
- **Button:** **ADD TO BAG — ONLY ONE** (espresso).
- **Description:** 3 sentences.
- **Measurements:** flat measurements table.
- **Delivery line:** “48–72 h · Cash on delivery or CCP / BaridiMob · Returns 7 days”.

### 7.3 Product cards
Photo first (4:5), “1 OF 1” badge top-left, then:
- title (Fraunces) and price (DM Sans Bold)
- era · size
- condition badge

## 8. Social media

### 8.1 Posting rhythm — *Build up to Friday*

| Day | Content |
|---|---|
| Mon | Story teaser: “something’s coming” |
| Wed | Post: product spotlight + price |
| Thu | Story: countdown sticker |
| **Fri 19:00** | **Post + stories: NEW DROP** |
| Sat | Story: SOLD stamps |
| Sun | Post: guide or values |

| Rule | Guideline |
|---|---|
| **Grid** | Alternate dark and light posts; one rust post per row. |
| **Captions** | Item · era · size · price · grade. Then one line of story. |
| **Claiming** | The first “claim” in DMs wins. Hold 2 hours, then release. |
| **Hashtags** | #vintagewave #thriftDZ #vintagealgerie + item and era |

### 8.2 Post templates (1080 × 1350)
1. **New drop:** espresso, “New *drop.*”, date, 4 product thumbnails, amber badge.
2. **Product spotlight:** big photo, “1 OF 1”, hand-written price, name, era/size, grade, “DM to claim”.
3. **Sold:** product photo, dark overlay, round “SOLD · FOUND A NEW HOME” stamp.
4. **Quote / values:** rust background: “The most sustainable piece is the one that *already exists.*”
5. **Carousel cover:** kraft, e.g. “How to spot real vintage *denim.*” · “SWIPE →”.

### 8.3 Stories (1080 × 1920)
1. **Just in:** photo, name, era/size, hand-written price, “LINK IN BIO”.
2. **This or that?:** two pieces side by side, with space for a poll sticker.
3. **Drop countdown:** rust, badge, “Drop in *3 days.*”, with space for a countdown sticker.
4. **Sold out:** “All *gone.*” plus a thank-you line.

### 8.4 Highlight covers
Espresso circles with cream icons: **New · Women · Men · Denim · Bags · Sold · Reviews · Shipping**

### 8.5 Profile picture
Cream symbol on espresso (alternatives: on rust, or espresso on cream).

## 9. Packaging & unboxing

*The moment the parcel opens is our shop window.*

### 9.1 The unboxing ritual — *Six steps, every order*
1. Fold the piece, hang tag on.
2. Wrap in wave tissue paper.
3. Seal with the badge sticker.
4. Add the hand-written thank-you card.
5. Add the care card.
6. Box or mailer, branded tape, shipping label.

### 9.2 Packaging items

| Item | Design |
|---|---|
| **Poly mailer** | Rust, stacked logo in cream, “*Something old is on its way.*”, shipping-label zone, “PEEL & SEAL · REUSE ME” |
| **Mailer box: lid** | Kraft, espresso frame, large badge |
| **Mailer box: inside** | Amber wave pattern on espresso, “*Hello, old friend.* · UNWRAP SLOWLY · IT HAS STORIES” |
| **Tissue paper** | Espresso wave pattern on cream |
| **Packing tape** | Rust, cream symbol + “SECOND LIFE INSIDE” |
| **Sticker sheet** | Badge · “pre-loved / RE-LOVED” · wordmark pill · “thank you for shopping second-hand” · symbol on denim · price sticker |

### 9.3 Tags & cards

| Item | Design |
|---|---|
| **Hang tag: front** | Espresso, stacked logo, “PRE-LOVED · RE-LOVED” |
| **Hang tag: back** | Cream, hand-filled fields: ITEM · ERA · SIZE · GRADE · PRICE |
| **Price sticker** | Round, amber, “PRICE”, hand-written amount, “DA” |
| **Thank-you card: front** | Espresso, “*Thank you* · FOR SHOPPING SECOND-HAND” |
| **Thank-you card: back** | Hand-written note (e.g. “Hi Sara, this jacket had a whole life before you — enjoy writing its next chapter. — Amel”) plus “Tag us @vintagewave · 10 % off your next piece: WAVE10” |
| **Care card** | Kraft: 01 Wash cold, inside out · 02 Air dry flat · 03 Mend, don’t bin · 04 Pass it on |
