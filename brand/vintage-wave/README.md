# Vintage Wave — Brand guidelines & kit

Online thrift and vintage store. Pre-loved pieces, new waves.

## The logo
A faithful vector rebuild of the original: a sun rising over two waves. The first wave is the life a piece already had; the second is the one it starts with its new owner. The hand-cut wordmark is kept exactly as drawn.

## Placeholders
Names, prices, the shop address (`vintagewave.shop`), the Instagram handle and the product photos are examples. The product "photos" are illustrated stand-ins; replace them with your own photos, following the photography rules in the guidelines.
